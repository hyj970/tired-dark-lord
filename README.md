A 100-word villain monologue for make.fun's "The Tired Dark Lord" bounty, $2 in USDC. The Dark Lord is interrupted mid-ritual by heroes at dawn — and would really rather they took the amulet and left, because the kingdom can wait until Tuesday.

> Heroes. Again. At dawn, naturally, because villains keep office hours too, apparently. I was halfway through the ritual, candles lit, the goat already complaining, when your lot kicked the door in. The door I had just finished painting. Black. Again. Look, take the amulet. It's in the drawer, yes, that one, beside the unpaid curses. The kingdom can wait until Tuesday; my knees have filed a formal complaint. And tell the bards to stop rhyming 'doom' with 'gloom': it scans terribly and I have standards, even at this hour. Now, quietly, please. The dragon is asleep and so am I.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the monologue to `out/entry.txt` — exactly 100 words, 581 characters.
